Connect Phone & Print Remotely

iPhone & iPad (Native AirPrint)

On your home Wi-Fi, AirPrint is completely zero-config:

  1. Open any photo, email, PDF, or document on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button → Tap Print.
  3. Select Epson EcoTank L3110 from the printer list and print!

Android (Print from Anywhere via Tailscale)

To print from your mobile device or desktop:

  1. Open phone Settings → Connected Devices → Printing.
  2. Tap Default Print Service (or Mopria Print Service).
  3. Tap the 3 dots (Menu) in top right → Add printer → Add printer by IP address.
  4. Enter: server-ip:631 or http://server-ip:631/printers/Epson_L3110
  5. Now whenever Tailscale or LAN is connected, select this printer in ANY app to print!
